Gentle robotics for people living with dementia

Kea Robotics builds assistive robots that bring calm, routine, and companionship to patients, and give caregivers the support they need.

Our mission

More than 55 million people worldwide live with dementia. We believe technology should meet them with patience and dignity. Our robots are designed alongside clinicians, families, and patients to support daily life without replacing the human connection at its heart.

How it works

🕰️

Daily routine support

Gentle reminders for meals, medication, and hydration, delivered in a familiar voice and at a comfortable pace.

💬

Companionship

Conversation, music, and reminiscence activities that reduce isolation and ease anxiety throughout the day.

🛡️

Safety monitoring

Non-intrusive awareness of falls, wandering, and distress, with immediate alerts to caregivers and family.

🤝

Caregiver relief

Insights and summaries that help caregivers understand patterns, plan care, and take a well-earned break.

Team
